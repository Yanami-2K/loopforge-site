# Approved homepage assets

These local assets were extracted from the supplied, approved homepage prototype.

- eclipse-corona.webp and eclipse-limb.webp: prepared exposures of NASA Artemis II photograph art002e009301, used for the opening eclipse and natural corona.
- terminator.webp: NASA Artemis II photograph art002e009283; final close-up plate.
- lunar-highlands.webp and designer-dissolve.webp: deployment photograph and graded transition crop from [Mountains of the Moon, PIA12927](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/mountains-of-the-moon/). Credit: NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University.
- terminator-relief.webp: full-frame opacity map derived from terminator.webp, registered to the same crop as the deployment photograph.
- regolith-in.webp, regolith-out.webp and regolith-paper.webp: transition masks and a low-contrast paper texture derived from terminator.webp.
- editorial.ttf: Instrument Serif, SIL Open Font License 1.1; license retained in licenses/InstrumentSerif.txt.
- ../../blog/assets/earthset.webp: NASA Artemis II photograph art002e009286, reused from the approved Journal design.
- QR and video posters reuse the repository's site/wechat-qr.png and demo/media/.

Photographic credit: NASA / Artemis II. Source references retained from the approved prototype: https://images.nasa.gov/details/art002e009301 , https://images.nasa.gov/details/art002e009283 , https://images.nasa.gov/details/art002e009286 .
